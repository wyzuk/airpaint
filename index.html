Hand Gesture Guide

Control AIRPAINT effortlessly with natural hand gestures in front of your camera.

☝️

1 Finger — Pen Mode

Raise only your index finger. Move your fingertip smoothly across the air to draw on the canvas.

✌️

2 Fingers — Eraser Mode

Raise your index and middle fingers together. Move across existing strokes to erase them cleanly.

🤟

3 Fingers — Color Palette

Raise 3 fingers (index, middle, ring). A floating palette appears; swipe your hand left or right to switch colors.

✋

5 Fingers — Open Palm (Pause)

Open your entire palm facing the camera to pause drawing and safely rest your hand without marking the canvas.

✊

Closed Fist — Idle Mode

Close your fist to enter idle mode. No drawing or erasing occurs.
